acerbilab

Tools for fitting computational models to data, from Luigi Acerbi's Machine and Human Intelligence Group at the University of Helsinki.

Fit your model

  • PyBADS

    Bayesian Adaptive Direct Search: fast, robust optimization for maximum-likelihood and MAP fits when the objective is rough, noisy or slow to evaluate.

    Docs Python MATLAB (BADS)

  • PyVBMC

    Variational Bayesian Monte Carlo: an approximate posterior over your parameters and an estimate of the model evidence, from a small budget of likelihood evaluations, even noisy ones.

    Docs Python MATLAB (VBMC)

  • PyIBS

    Inverse binomial sampling: unbiased, efficient estimates of the log-likelihood of models you can simulate but not write down, for data with discrete responses.

    Python MATLAB (IBS)

Next wave

  • ACE

    The Amortized Conditioning Engine: one transformer, trained on simulated data, that answers inference and prediction questions in a single forward pass. Research code in active development.

    Paper nanoACE playground
